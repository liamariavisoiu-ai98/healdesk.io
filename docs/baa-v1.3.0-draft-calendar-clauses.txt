HEALDESK BUSINESS ASSOCIATE AGREEMENT
Version 1.3.0 — executed electronically

This Agreement is entered into under 45 CFR 164.502(e) and 164.504(e) and
takes effect on the date below. It governs Protected Health Information
that healdesk creates, receives, maintains or transmits for Practice in the
course of the Services defined in the Terms.

BETWEEN
  Golden Clinic LLC ("Practice"), a med-spa practice
  Viixi FZC, a company registered under the Sharjah Publishing City Free Zone,
  United Arab Emirates ("healdesk"), of 800 N King Street, Suite 304-1426,
  Wilmington, DE 19801, United States, and by email to legal@healdesk.io

SIGNED FOR PRACTICE BY
  Alex Chen, Owner
  alex@golden.test

EFFECTIVE 13 September 2026

1. DEFINITIONS

  "Practice" means Golden Clinic LLC. "healdesk" means Viixi FZC, a company
  registered under the Sharjah Publishing City Free Zone, United Arab
  Emirates. "Terms" means the healdesk Terms of Service at healdesk.io/terms
  as in force on the effective date. "Services" and "Fees" have the meanings
  the Terms give them. "Protected health information" (PHI), "breach",
  "unsecured PHI", "security incident", "subcontractor" and "designated record
  set" have the meanings given in 45 CFR Parts 160 and 164. "Business day"
  means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or United States federal holiday.

  This agreement governs PHI healdesk creates, receives, maintains or
  transmits for Practice in the course of the Services. Where this agreement
  and the Terms conflict about PHI, this agreement governs.

2. PRACTICE'S STATUS

  Practice has notified healdesk that it transmits health information
  electronically in connection with a transaction covered by HIPAA and is
  therefore a covered entity.

  Practice's status is Practice's determination, not healdesk's. Whatever it
  is, healdesk applies the safeguards in this agreement, and this agreement is
  a business associate agreement under 45 CFR 164.502(e) and 164.504(e) where
  Practice is a covered entity, and a data-protection agreement on the same
  terms where it is not. Practice will tell healdesk promptly if its status
  changes. Where Practice is not a covered entity, state breach-notification
  and consumer-health-privacy laws may apply to both parties, and in some
  states carry a private right of action.

3. WHAT HEALDESK MAY DO WITH PATIENT INFORMATION

  healdesk may use and disclose protected health information only as necessary
  to provide the service to Golden Clinic LLC, and as required by law. It may
  not use or disclose it in any way that Golden Clinic LLC could not.

4. WHERE PRACTICE IS NOT A COVERED ENTITY

  This clause applies for any period in which Practice is not a covered
  entity, whatever Practice has told healdesk about its status. For that
  period, "patient information" means any information about an identified or
  identifiable individual, other than Practice's own staff, that healdesk
  collects, receives or creates for Practice in the course of the Services,
  including all that would be PHI if Practice were a covered entity, and
  everything this agreement says about PHI applies to it.

  healdesk processes patient information only on Practice's documented
  instructions, which are the Terms and Practice's portal settings, and for no
  other purpose. It does not sell or share it, does not retain, use or
  disclose it outside the direct business relationship with Practice or for
  any purpose other than providing the Services, and does not combine it with
  information from any other source except as the Services require. healdesk
  will give Practice the information it needs to answer a consumer's access,
  deletion or correction request within ten business days; will tell Practice
  if it can no longer meet these obligations; will let Practice take
  reasonable steps to stop and remedy any unauthorised use; and will provide,
  on request, the documents Practice reasonably needs to verify compliance.
  The reporting, subcontractor, security and return-or-destroy clauses of this
  agreement apply to that information as they apply to PHI.

  healdesk is Practice's service provider and processor under the California
  Consumer Privacy Act and its regulations (Cal. Civ. Code 1798.100 and
  following; 11 CCR 7000 and following), the Washington My Health My Data Act
  (RCW 19.373), Nevada's consumer health data law (NRS 603A.400 to 603A.550)
  and the Connecticut Data Privacy Act (Conn. Gen. Stat. 42-515 to 42-526), to
  the extent each applies (together, "the state privacy laws"). Where this
  clause and another clause of this agreement differ about patient
  information, this clause governs, and any ambiguity is resolved in favour of
  a meaning that permits compliance with the state privacy laws.

  Practice makes patient information available to healdesk, and healdesk
  collects it for Practice, only for the limited and specified purposes below,
  which, with the settings Practice chooses in its portal, are Practice's
  instructions. healdesk processes patient information only to:

    a. answer, in Practice's name, the calls, website chats and Instagram,
       Messenger and WhatsApp messages that people start with Practice, from
       Practice's own services, prices, hours and policies, using a language
       model to understand each message and write each reply;
    b. check Practice's calendar, hold a time and add the appointment, or take
       a booking request for Practice's staff to confirm;
    c. send Practice's own payment link where Practice takes deposits;
    d. pass urgent messages and requests for a person to the contacts Practice
       names;
    e. keep transcripts and records of those conversations and bookings, show
       them to Practice's staff, and export or delete them when Practice asks;
    f. count conversations, minutes and bookings, to bill Practice and to
       report them to it;
    g. read a conversation that Practice flags, or that a fault or a complaint
       makes it necessary to read, to correct the assistant for Practice; and
    h. keep the Services secure, find and fix faults, detect and prevent fraud
       and abuse, and meet healdesk's own legal obligations.

  The patient information involved is names, telephone numbers, email
  addresses and messaging-account identifiers; what people say or write, kept
  as transcripts with no call audio; the services and times they ask about;
  booking and deposit status; free and busy times on Practice's calendar and
  the appointments healdesk adds to it; and the number or network address a
  call or chat comes from. healdesk processes it while Practice uses the
  Services, keeps it no longer than the periods this agreement states, and
  when the Services end returns or destroys it as the Term clause provides.
  Afterwards it keeps only archived or backup copies until they expire, within
  six months at most, and what a law requires it to keep or the Subcontractors
  clause says may be kept for security and abuse detection, each for that
  purpose and that period only.

  healdesk will not sell or share patient information, as Cal. Civ. Code
  1798.140 defines those words; will not retain, use or disclose it for any
  purpose other than those above, including any other commercial purpose, or
  outside the direct business relationship between healdesk and Practice,
  except as a state privacy law expressly permits; and will not combine it
  with personal information that it receives from or for anyone else, or
  collects from its own interaction with a person, except to keep the Services
  secure and detect fraud and abuse, or to produce the de-identified
  statistics the Own use clause permits. healdesk makes no such statistics
  from consumer health data as RCW 19.373.010 or NRS 603A.430 defines it,
  keeps them de-identified, makes no attempt to re-identify them, and requires
  by contract anyone who receives them to do the same. healdesk understands
  these restrictions and will comply with them.

  healdesk will comply with the obligations the state privacy laws place on it
  in that role, and give patient information the same level of privacy
  protection they require of Practice. It will:

    a. ensure that every person who processes patient information for it is
       bound by a duty of confidentiality, and give access only to those who
       need it for the purposes above;
    b. help Practice, by appropriate technical and organisational measures and
       insofar as it is possible, to meet its obligations under those laws,
       including answering the requests people make under them, giving
       Practice what it needs within ten business days of being asked;
    c. when Practice tells it to delete a person's information, delete it from
       healdesk's own records within ten business days and from archived or
       backup copies as they expire and in any case within six months, have
       each subcontractor that holds it delete it in time for Practice to meet
       the deadline the law sets, and keep only a record of the request, used
       for nothing but making sure the information stays deleted;
    d. give Practice the information it holds that Practice needs for any risk
       assessment, data protection or impact assessment, or cybersecurity
       audit those laws require of Practice, and misrepresent no fact relevant
       to one;
    e. tell Practice promptly after it determines that it can no longer meet
       its obligations under this clause; and
    f. notify Practice of a breach immediately following discovery wherever a
       state breach-notification law requires a person holding data for its
       owner to notify the owner that soon, by email to the address in the
       Notices clause, then report as the Reporting clause provides, and help
       Practice meet its own duties to secure patient information and to give
       notice of a breach.

  Practice may take reasonable and appropriate steps to ensure that healdesk
  uses patient information consistently with Practice's obligations under the
  state privacy laws and, on notice, to stop and remedy any unauthorised use,
  including requiring documents showing that healdesk has deleted what it was
  told to delete. On reasonable request healdesk will make available all the
  information in its possession necessary to show that it meets this clause.
  Practice, or an assessor Practice designates, may assess healdesk's
  compliance with this clause on reasonable notice, once in any twelve months
  and after any breach, at Practice's cost; healdesk will cooperate, or may
  instead have a qualified and independent assessor assess its policies and
  technical and organisational measures against an appropriate and accepted
  control standard or framework, and will give Practice the report on request.

  The Subcontractors clause applies to patient information: its 24 hours'
  notice is Practice's opportunity to object, and the written agreement it
  requires binds each subcontractor to the obligations of this clause. Google
  Calendar, Instagram, Messenger, WhatsApp and Practice's payment processor
  are services Practice connects under its own accounts with their providers.
  They are not healdesk's subcontractors, and healdesk sends patient
  information to them only to answer people on Instagram, Messenger and
  WhatsApp and to add appointments to the calendar. At termination the record
  of disclosures that the Term clause keeps for six years goes to Practice
  with the export, and healdesk keeps no copy of it that contains patient
  information unless a law requires it.

  Where Practice is a provider of health care under California's
  Confidentiality of Medical Information Act, healdesk receives medical
  information from Practice under Cal. Civ. Code 56.10(c)(3) and will not
  further disclose it in a way that would violate that Act.

  Practice will tell healdesk of any request under the state privacy laws that
  healdesk must act on, with the information healdesk needs to act on it; will
  make sure its own notices, consents and consumer health data privacy policy
  cover the processing this clause describes; and will not instruct healdesk
  to process patient information in a way those laws forbid. A request made to
  healdesk directly is handled as the Patient access clause provides.

5. WHAT HEALDESK READS FROM YOUR SYSTEMS

  healdesk holds no credentials to any practice-management or point-of-sale
  system and reads nothing from one. The only patient information healdesk
  receives is what patients themselves give it during a call or chat, together
  with the calendar access the Google Calendar clause describes.

6. PRACTICE'S GOOGLE CALENDAR

  Practice connects its own Google Calendar by OAuth, under Practice's own
  agreement with Google; healdesk uses one calendar of the Google account
  Practice connects, which is that account's main calendar unless Practice
  chooses another calendar the account owns. Practice has told healdesk that
  this account belongs to Practice's Google Workspace organisation and that
  its administrator has accepted Google's business associate agreement, and
  Practice will record a calendar election promptly if that stops being true.
  healdesk cannot see whether Google's agreement was accepted and relies on
  Practice's statement; it adds nothing to a calendar whose Google account
  does not belong to a Google Workspace or Cloud Identity organisation.

  healdesk reads the names of the calendars that account owns, so that
  Practice can choose one; when that calendar is free or busy; and the
  appointments healdesk itself added to it. Google's permission model also
  gives healdesk access to the other events on the calendars that account
  owns, and to when the other calendars it can see are free or busy; healdesk
  does not read or store them. healdesk holds the OAuth token encrypted and
  uses it for nothing else. It stops using the token when this agreement ends,
  and deletes it when Practice disconnects the calendar through healdesk, when
  healdesk finds that Practice has removed healdesk's access in its Google
  Account, and in any case when healdesk destroys Practice's data at the end
  of this agreement.

  healdesk adds an appointment to that calendar when the assistant books one,
  and when Practice's staff confirm a booking request through healdesk. Each
  appointment carries the patient's first name, the service asked about as
  Practice's own service list names it, and the time, and no other information
  about the patient: never a telephone number, an email address or anything
  clinical. healdesk marks each appointment private, invites no one to it and
  sets no reminders on it, and it moves, edits or cancels nothing it did not
  create. Google keeps a deleted event in that calendar's trash for up to 30
  days under Google's own terms.

  Practice acknowledges that Google's business associate agreement is between
  Practice and Google and does not extend to applications Practice authorises,
  and that healdesk's access to the calendar is governed by this agreement.

  This clause governs the calendar of the location Practice sets up when it
  signs this agreement. For any other location, and for that location once
  Practice changes the kind of Google account it uses or its booking system,
  the calendar election Practice records for that location takes the place of
  this clause for that location.

7. PAYMENTS

  Where Practice takes deposits, healdesk creates a payment link through
  Practice's own payment processor. The link and the payment carry an amount,
  a generic description and a reference number, and no service name or
  clinical detail, including in metadata and the statement descriptor.
  healdesk never takes card numbers.

8. HEALDESK'S OWN USE, AND DE-IDENTIFIED STATISTICS

  healdesk may use PHI where necessary for its own proper management and
  administration and to carry out its legal responsibilities, and may disclose
  it for those purposes only where the law requires the disclosure, or where
  healdesk has obtained reasonable assurances from the recipient that the PHI
  will be held confidentially, used or further disclosed only as required by
  law or for the purpose it was disclosed for, and that the recipient will
  notify healdesk of any breach of its confidentiality. 45 CFR 164.504(e)(4).

  healdesk may de-identify Practice's PHI in accordance with 45 CFR
  164.514(b), and combine the de-identified information with de-identified
  information from other practices, only to produce aggregate statistics about
  the Services (for example, what fraction of after-hours calls are answered).
  It will not re-identify, or try to re-identify, any individual from that
  information. It will not use PHI to train, fine-tune or evaluate any model,
  and nothing in this clause permits it to.

9. WHAT PRACTICE WILL TELL HEALDESK

  Practice will tell healdesk, promptly and in writing:

    a. of any limitation in its notice of privacy practices that affects how
       healdesk may use or disclose PHI;
    b. of any change to, or revocation of, a patient's permission to use or
       disclose PHI, where it affects healdesk;
    c. of any restriction on the use or disclosure of PHI that Practice has
       agreed to under 45 CFR 164.522, where it affects healdesk.

  Practice will not ask healdesk to use or disclose PHI in a way that would
  not be permissible under the HIPAA Privacy Rule if done by Practice.

10. SAFEGUARDS

  healdesk will use appropriate administrative, physical and technical
  safeguards to prevent any use or disclosure of PHI this agreement does not
  permit, and will comply with Subpart C of 45 CFR Part 164 — the HIPAA
  Security Rule — in respect of electronic PHI.

  PHI is held on United States infrastructure and encrypted at rest. In
  transit it is encrypted wherever healdesk controls both ends, and healdesk
  will not transmit PHI over a channel that cannot negotiate encryption. An
  email whose receiving server will not negotiate encryption is failed, not
  sent, and healdesk tells Practice rather than send it in the clear; the list
  of individuals in a breach report travels by secure transfer, never in the
  email itself.

  One narrow exception: a patient who telephones has chosen the telephone, and
  a telephone call is not end-to-end encrypted. healdesk may answer such a
  call, limited to the minimum necessary and never including clinical detail,
  test results or payment information. healdesk places no calls and sends no
  SMS. This exception does not extend to any other channel or content.

11. MINIMUM NECESSARY

  Each party will limit its use, disclosure and requests of PHI to the minimum
  necessary to accomplish the purpose, consistent with 45 CFR 164.502(b).

  The assistant collects what a receptionist would write on a callback slip —
  a name, a telephone number or email address, the service asked about and a
  preferred time — and no more. It does not ask for a history, a medication or
  a diagnosis, and it never takes a card number. On every channel its opening
  line says that it is an AI assistant and, on a call, that the call is
  transcribed; Practice cannot switch that statement off or edit it out.

  healdesk keeps transcripts and conversation records for 90 days, which
  Practice may shorten in its portal; no call audio is kept at all. The Term
  clause governs what is held after termination.

12. REPORTING BREACHES AND SECURITY INCIDENTS

  healdesk will report to Practice, in writing to the address in the Notices
  clause:

    a. any use or disclosure of PHI not permitted by this agreement, within
       five business days of healdesk becoming aware of it;
    b. any breach of unsecured PHI, without unreasonable delay and in no case
       later than five calendar days after discovery. A breach is treated as
       discovered on the first day it is known, or by exercising reasonable
       diligence would have been known, to healdesk or to any person other
       than the one committing it who is healdesk's workforce member or agent,
       as 45 CFR 164.410(a)(2) provides. Five days is deliberately far inside
       the sixty the regulation allows, because Practice's own sixty-day clock
       to notify patients may run from healdesk's discovery, not from
       healdesk's report;
    c. any security incident, within five business days of becoming aware of
       it — except routine unsuccessful attempts that compromised nothing,
       such as blocked sign-ins, port scans and filtered traffic, which the
       parties agree this clause reports once, here, and which healdesk will
       summarise on request.

  A breach report will include, to the extent known and supplemented as
  learned: the identification of each individual whose unsecured PHI was or is
  reasonably believed to have been accessed, acquired, used or disclosed; a
  description of what happened, including the dates of the breach and of its
  discovery; the types of PHI involved; what healdesk is doing to investigate,
  mitigate and prevent recurrence; and a contact. 45 CFR 164.410(c). Who bears
  the cost of notifying patients, regulators and anyone else about a breach is
  allocated by the Liability clause.

13. SUBCONTRACTORS

  healdesk uses subcontractors to deliver the Services — telephony, speech
  recognition, language models, hosting and email among them. healdesk will
  ensure that any subcontractor that creates, receives, maintains or transmits
  PHI on healdesk's behalf agrees in writing, before it does so, to the same
  restrictions and conditions this agreement places on healdesk, and to comply
  with the HIPAA Security Rule, under a contract meeting 45 CFR 164.314(a) and
  164.504(e)(5). healdesk remains answerable to Practice for its
  subcontractors' acts and omissions with that PHI.

  Language models are used to understand a patient and answer them during a
  conversation. healdesk's language-model provider is Anthropic, which
  receives the content of each conversation to generate the assistant's
  replies, under a business associate agreement with healdesk. Under that
  agreement Anthropic retains conversation content for 30 days, for abuse
  detection and security, and then deletes it, except content its automated
  trust-and-safety systems flag or the law requires it to keep, which it may
  retain for up to two years; its covered service cannot run with shorter
  retention. Anthropic never trains on that content, and healdesk may not use
  PHI to train, fine-tune or evaluate any model, its own or a third party's.

  healdesk keeps a current list of its subcontractors at
  healdesk.io/subprocessors and will give Practice at least 24 hours' notice
  by email before adding one that will handle the information this agreement
  protects. If Practice objects on reasonable grounds before the change takes
  effect, Practice may terminate this agreement without penalty.

14. PATIENT ACCESS, AMENDMENT AND ACCOUNTING

  When a patient asks to see their information, to have it corrected, or to be
  told who it was disclosed to, Golden Clinic LLC answers them — and healdesk
  gives Golden Clinic LLC what it needs to answer, within ten business days of
  being asked:

    a. Access. healdesk will make available the protected health information
       it holds about that patient in a designated record set, so Golden
       Clinic LLC can meet its obligations under 45 CFR 164.524.
    b. Amendment. healdesk will make that information available for amendment
       and will incorporate any amendment Golden Clinic LLC directs, under 45
       CFR 164.526. Incorporating it means healdesk's own copy is corrected,
       not merely annotated.
    c. Accounting. healdesk will maintain and make available the information
       Golden Clinic LLC needs to give a patient an accounting of disclosures
       under 45 CFR 164.528.

  If a patient makes one of these requests to healdesk directly, healdesk will
  not answer it. It will pass the request to Golden Clinic LLC within five
  business days and tell the patient it has done so.

15. WHERE HEALDESK ACTS IN YOUR PLACE

  Some of what healdesk does is Golden Clinic LLC carrying out its own duties
  through healdesk, rather than healdesk doing something of its own —
  answering a patient who calls, and replying in Golden Clinic LLC's name to a
  patient who writes, are both of those. Where healdesk carries out an
  obligation the HIPAA Privacy Rule places on Golden Clinic LLC, healdesk will
  meet the requirements that Rule places on Golden Clinic LLC in performing
  it, and not only the lighter ones it places on a business associate. 45 CFR
  164.504(e)(2)(ii)(H).

16. WHAT HEALDESK IS NOT AUTHORISED TO DO

  · Use PHI to train, fine-tune, or evaluate any artificial intelligence
    model, its own or a third party's.
  · Disclose PHI to any advertising or analytics platform, in any form,
    including hashed or aggregated. Instagram, Messenger and WhatsApp are
    operated by Meta, which signs no business associate agreement; where
    Practice connects them, the assistant replies there only inside a
    conversation the patient started, handles no clinical content, confirms no
    appointment details, and moves anything clinical to a call or to
    Practice's own secure route.
  · Provide clinical advice, diagnosis, triage, or treatment recommendations
    through the assistant.
  · Sell, license, or otherwise monetise patient information.

17. TERM, TERMINATION, AND WHAT HAPPENS TO THE INFORMATION

  This agreement takes effect on the effective date and continues for as long
  as healdesk holds any PHI for Practice. Either party may terminate it on
  written notice if the other materially breaches it and does not cure the
  breach within thirty days of being told of it in writing; where cure is not
  possible, on notice. Practice may also terminate under the
  subcontractor-objection right above. Ending the Services ends this
  agreement, subject to the rest of this clause.

  Within thirty days of termination healdesk will return or destroy all PHI it
  holds for Practice and retain no copies, and will give Practice, on request
  before that date, an export of the PHI it holds in a usable electronic form.
  Where Practice has asked for an export that has not been delivered by then,
  healdesk will destroy the PHI as soon as the export is delivered, and in no
  case later than ninety days after termination; until then this agreement
  continues to protect it. Where return or destruction is not feasible,
  healdesk will say why in writing, extend the protections of this agreement
  to that PHI for as long as it is held, and limit further use to what makes
  return or destruction infeasible.

  Two records survive termination: the signed copy of this agreement, and the
  record of disclosures needed to answer an accounting request, each kept for
  six years from termination as 45 CFR 164.530(j) and 164.528 require. Neither
  contains more PHI than the record itself needs.

18. LIABILITY, AND WHO INDEMNIFIES WHOM

  To the fullest extent the law allows, healdesk is not liable to Practice for
  any loss arising out of or relating to this agreement or the Services, in
  contract, tort or otherwise, including lost profits and indirect,
  incidental, special or consequential damages. Where the law does not permit
  that exclusion, healdesk's total aggregate liability to Practice, across all
  claims, is limited to the Fees paid or payable by Practice in the twelve
  months before the event giving rise to the claim. That limit does not apply
  to healdesk's wilful misconduct or gross negligence, or to any liability the
  law does not allow to be limited.

  Practice will defend, indemnify and hold harmless healdesk and its
  subcontractors as section 14 of the Terms provides: against every claim,
  loss, liability, penalty, fine, cost and expense — including reasonable
  legal fees, the cost of defending a claim, and the cost of investigating a
  security incident and of notifying patients, regulators, the media and
  anyone else about a breach — to the extent it arises from or relates to the
  Services, PHI that Practice or its patients give the Services, Practice's
  covered-entity status, instructions Practice gave, Practice's own breach of
  this agreement or violation of HIPAA or of state consumer-health-privacy
  law, or a claim by a patient, a regulator or any other third party. This
  indemnity applies to the fullest extent the law allows, including where the
  claim alleges negligence on healdesk's part, and the limit above does not
  apply to it. healdesk gives Practice no indemnity.

  This clause allocates cost between the parties. It does not change what each
  party owes a regulator directly under HIPAA: nothing in it relieves healdesk
  of a civil money penalty a regulator imposes on healdesk itself, which no
  contract can shift, and Practice's own duty under 45 CFR 164.404 to notify
  its patients remains Practice's.

  healdesk does not carry liability insurance, and its own suppliers cap their
  liability to healdesk at figures far below what a breach can cost. Practice
  should size its own cover accordingly.

19. AVAILABILITY OF RECORDS

  healdesk will make its internal practices, books and records relating to the
  use and disclosure of protected health information available to the
  Secretary of Health and Human Services for the purpose of determining
  compliance.
