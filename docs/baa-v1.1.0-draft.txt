HEALDESK BUSINESS ASSOCIATE AGREEMENT
Version 1.1.0 — DRAFT FOR COUNSEL REVIEW — NOT FOR EXECUTION

This Agreement is entered into under 45 CFR 164.502(e) and 164.504(e) and
takes effect on the date below. It governs Protected Health Information
that healdesk creates, receives, maintains or transmits for Practice in the
course of the Services defined in the Terms.

BETWEEN
  Golden Clinic LLC ("Practice"), a med-spa practice
  Viixi FZC, a free zone company incorporated in the United Arab Emirates, trading as healdesk ("healdesk"), of [REGISTERED ADDRESS — from the Stripe account, Settings → Business details], legal@healdesk.io

SIGNED FOR PRACTICE BY
  Alex Chen, Owner
  alex@golden.test

ACCEPTED FOR HEALDESK BY
  [NAME, TITLE] — healdesk's acceptance is given by its countersignature
  record, sent to the signatory's email with the executed copy.

EFFECTIVE 13 September 2026

1. DEFINITIONS

  "Practice" means Golden Clinic LLC. "healdesk" means Viixi FZC, a free zone
  company incorporated in the United Arab Emirates, trading as healdesk.
  "Terms" means the healdesk Terms of Service at healdesk.io/terms as in force
  on the effective date. "Services" and "Fees" have the meanings the Terms
  give them. "Protected health information" (PHI), "breach", "unsecured PHI",
  "security incident", "subcontractor" and "designated record set" have the
  meanings given in 45 CFR Parts 160 and 164. "Business day" means a day other
  than a Saturday, Sunday or United States federal holiday.

  This agreement governs PHI healdesk creates, receives, maintains or
  transmits for Practice in the course of the Services. Where this agreement
  and the Terms conflict about PHI, this agreement governs.

2. PRACTICE'S STATUS

  Practice has not yet notified healdesk whether it is a covered entity, and
  will do so before any real patient information is processed.

  Practice's status is Practice's determination, not healdesk's. Whatever it
  is, healdesk applies the safeguards in this agreement, and this agreement is
  a business associate agreement under 45 CFR 164.502(e) and 164.504(e) where
  Practice is a covered entity, and a data-protection agreement on the same
  terms where it is not. Practice will tell healdesk promptly if its status
  changes. Where Practice is not a covered entity, state
  consumer-health-privacy law may apply to both parties and in some states
  carries a private right of action; the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule
  may apply to Practice.

3. WHAT HEALDESK MAY DO WITH PATIENT INFORMATION

  healdesk may use and disclose protected health information only as necessary
  to provide the service to Golden Clinic LLC, and as required by law. It may
  not use or disclose it in any way that Golden Clinic LLC could not.

4. WHAT HEALDESK READS FROM YOUR SYSTEMS

  Golden Clinic LLC has chosen estimated reporting. healdesk holds no
  credentials to any practice management system and reads nothing from it. The
  only patient information healdesk receives is what patients themselves give
  it during a call or chat. Revenue figures are calculated from the average
  appointment value Golden Clinic LLC supplied, and are labelled as estimates
  wherever they appear.

5. HEALDESK'S OWN USE, AND DATA AGGREGATION

  healdesk may use PHI for its own proper management and administration and to
  carry out its legal responsibilities, and may disclose it for those purposes
  only where the law requires the disclosure, or where healdesk has obtained
  reasonable assurances from the recipient that the PHI will be held
  confidentially, used or further disclosed only as required by law or for the
  purpose it was disclosed for, and that the recipient will notify healdesk of
  any breach of its confidentiality. 45 CFR 164.504(e)(4).

  healdesk may combine Practice's PHI with that of other practices only to
  produce de-identified, aggregate statistics about the Services (for example,
  what fraction of after-hours calls are answered). It will not use PHI to
  train, fine-tune or evaluate any model, and nothing in this clause permits
  it to.

6. WHAT PRACTICE WILL TELL HEALDESK

  Practice will tell healdesk, promptly and in writing:

    a. of any limitation in its notice of privacy practices that affects how
       healdesk may use or disclose PHI;
    b. of any change to, or revocation of, a patient's permission to use or
       disclose PHI, where it affects healdesk;
    c. of any restriction on the use or disclosure of PHI that Practice has
       agreed to under 45 CFR 164.522, where it affects healdesk.

  Practice will not ask healdesk to use or disclose PHI in a way that would
  not be permissible under the HIPAA Privacy Rule if done by Practice.

7. SAFEGUARDS

  healdesk will use appropriate administrative, physical and technical
  safeguards to prevent any use or disclosure of PHI this agreement does not
  permit, and will comply with Subpart C of 45 CFR Part 164 — the HIPAA
  Security Rule — in respect of electronic PHI.

  PHI is held on United States infrastructure and encrypted at rest. In
  transit it is encrypted wherever healdesk controls both ends, and healdesk
  will not transmit PHI over a channel that cannot negotiate encryption —
  email included. Where a message cannot be delivered securely healdesk will
  fail the delivery and tell Practice, rather than send it in the clear.

  One narrow exception: a patient who telephones, or who asks to be reached by
  text message at a number they gave, has chosen that channel, and a telephone
  call or an SMS is not end-to-end encrypted. healdesk may answer a call and
  may send an appointment confirmation, reminder or missed-call follow-up by
  SMS to a number the patient supplied for that purpose, limited to the
  minimum necessary and never including clinical detail, test results or
  payment information. This exception does not extend to any other channel or
  content.

8. MINIMUM NECESSARY

  Each party will limit its use, disclosure and requests of PHI to the minimum
  necessary to accomplish the purpose, consistent with 45 CFR 164.502(b).
  healdesk's agent asks a caller for no more than is needed to book, message
  or route them; it does not ask for a diagnosis, a history or a reason beyond
  the service requested.

9. REPORTING BREACHES AND SECURITY INCIDENTS

  healdesk will report to Practice, in writing to the address in the Notices
  clause:

    a. any use or disclosure of PHI not permitted by this agreement, within
       five business days of healdesk becoming aware of it;
    b. any breach of unsecured PHI, without unreasonable delay and in no case
       later than five calendar days after discovery. A breach is treated as
       discovered on the first day it is known, or by exercising reasonable
       diligence would have been known, to healdesk or to any person other
       than the one committing it who is healdesk's workforce member or agent,
       as 45 CFR 164.410(a)(2) provides. Five days is deliberately far inside
       the sixty the regulation allows, because Practice's own sixty-day clock
       to notify patients may run from healdesk's discovery, not from
       healdesk's report;
    c. any security incident, within five business days of becoming aware of
       it — except routine unsuccessful attempts that compromised nothing,
       such as blocked sign-ins, port scans and filtered traffic, which the
       parties agree this clause reports once, here, and which healdesk will
       summarise on request.

  A breach report will include, to the extent known and supplemented as
  learned: the identification of each individual whose unsecured PHI was or is
  reasonably believed to have been accessed, acquired, used or disclosed; a
  description of what happened, including the dates of the breach and of its
  discovery; the types of PHI involved; what healdesk is doing to investigate,
  mitigate and prevent recurrence; and a contact. 45 CFR 164.410(c). The cost
  of notifying patients, regulators and anyone else about a breach is
  Practice's, as the Liability clause provides.

10. SUBCONTRACTORS

  healdesk uses subcontractors to deliver the Services — telephony, speech
  recognition, language models, hosting and email among them. healdesk will
  ensure that any subcontractor that creates, receives, maintains or transmits
  PHI on healdesk's behalf agrees in writing, before it does so, to the same
  restrictions and conditions this agreement places on healdesk, and to comply
  with the HIPAA Security Rule, under a contract meeting 45 CFR 164.314(a) and
  164.504(e)(5). healdesk remains answerable to Practice for its
  subcontractors' acts and omissions with that PHI.

  Language models are used to understand a caller and answer them during a
  conversation, under a business associate agreement with the model provider.
  healdesk does not permit, and its provider agreements prohibit, the use of
  PHI to train or improve any model, and healdesk configures each provider to
  retain conversation content no longer than that provider's agreement with
  healdesk requires for abuse detection and security, and never for any other
  purpose.

  healdesk keeps a current list of these subcontractors at
  healdesk.io/subprocessors and will give Practice at least fifteen days'
  notice by email before adding one that will handle PHI. If Practice objects
  on reasonable grounds before the change takes effect, Practice may terminate
  under the Term clause without penalty.

11. PATIENT ACCESS, AMENDMENT AND ACCOUNTING

  When a patient asks to see their information, to have it corrected, or to be
  told who it was disclosed to, Golden Clinic LLC answers them — and healdesk
  gives Golden Clinic LLC what it needs to answer, within ten business days of
  being asked:

    a. Access. healdesk will make available the protected health information
       it holds about that patient in a designated record set, so Golden
       Clinic LLC can meet its obligations under 45 CFR 164.524.
    b. Amendment. healdesk will make that information available for amendment
       and will incorporate any amendment Golden Clinic LLC directs, under 45
       CFR 164.526. Incorporating it means healdesk's own copy is corrected,
       not merely annotated.
    c. Accounting. healdesk will maintain and make available the information
       Golden Clinic LLC needs to give a patient an accounting of disclosures
       under 45 CFR 164.528.

  If a patient makes one of these requests to healdesk directly, healdesk will
  not answer it. It will pass the request to Golden Clinic LLC within five
  business days and tell the patient it has done so.

12. WHERE HEALDESK ACTS IN YOUR PLACE

  Some of what healdesk does is Golden Clinic LLC carrying out its own duties
  through healdesk, rather than healdesk doing something of its own —
  answering a patient who calls, and sending messages in Golden Clinic LLC's
  name, are both of those. Where healdesk carries out an obligation the HIPAA
  Privacy Rule places on Golden Clinic LLC, healdesk will meet the
  requirements that Rule places on Golden Clinic LLC in performing it, and not
  only the lighter ones it places on a business associate. 45 CFR
  164.504(e)(2)(ii)(H).

13. OUTBOUND CONTACT, AND WHO HOLDS THE PATIENTS' CONSENT

  healdesk sends appointment reminders, confirmations, missed-call follow-ups
  and reactivation messages to Golden Clinic LLC's patients, by call and by
  text, on Golden Clinic LLC's behalf and in its name.

  By signing, Golden Clinic LLC warrants that:

    a. It holds the consents required to contact those patients for the
       purposes above, obtained from the patients themselves.
    b. Its consent for promotional or reactivation messaging is prior express
       WRITTEN consent, which is the standard the law applies to marketing
       regardless of how the message is worded.
    c. It authorises healdesk to send those messages as its agent.
    d. It will tell healdesk promptly when a patient withdraws consent by any
       route — in the clinic, by phone, or in writing.

  healdesk will, on every message and every call: honour STOP and any other
  plainly worded withdrawal, suppress the number across all channels, respect
  quiet hours in the patient's own local time, and hold to the frequency
  limits the healthcare exemption requires.

14. REACTIVATION MESSAGING IS NOT SWITCHED ON

  The healthcare exemption relied on above is available only to a HIPAA
  covered entity or its business associate. Because the covered-entity
  determination for Golden Clinic LLC is "not yet determined", healdesk will
  NOT switch on reactivation or win-back messaging until that question is
  settled. Appointment reminders and missed-call follow-ups are unaffected.

15. WHAT HEALDESK IS NOT AUTHORISED TO DO

  · Use protected health information to train, fine-tune, or evaluate any
    artificial intelligence model, its own or a third party's.
  · Disclose protected health information to any advertising platform, in any
    form, including hashed or aggregated.
  · Provide clinical advice, diagnosis, triage, or treatment recommendations
    through the agent.
  · Sell, license, or otherwise monetise patient information.

16. TERM, TERMINATION, AND WHAT HAPPENS TO THE INFORMATION

  This agreement takes effect on the effective date and continues for as long
  as healdesk holds any PHI for Practice. Either party may terminate it on
  written notice if the other materially breaches it and does not cure the
  breach within thirty days of being told of it in writing; where cure is not
  possible, on notice. Practice may also terminate under the
  subcontractor-objection right above. Ending the Services ends this
  agreement, subject to the rest of this clause.

  Within thirty days of termination healdesk will return or destroy all PHI it
  holds for Practice and retain no copies, and will give Practice, on request
  before that date, an export of the PHI it holds in a usable electronic form.
  Where return or destruction is not feasible, healdesk will say why in
  writing, extend the protections of this agreement to that PHI for as long as
  it is held, and limit further use to what makes return or destruction
  infeasible.

  Two records survive termination: the signed copy of this agreement, and the
  record of disclosures needed to answer an accounting request, each kept for
  six years from termination as 45 CFR 164.530(j) and 164.528 require. Neither
  contains more PHI than the record itself needs.

17. LIABILITY, AND WHO INDEMNIFIES WHOM

  Practice will defend, indemnify and hold harmless healdesk and its
  subcontractors against every claim, loss, liability, regulatory penalty,
  fine, cost and expense — including reasonable legal fees, the cost of
  defending a claim, and the cost of investigating a security incident and of
  notifying patients, regulators, the media and anyone else about a breach —
  to the extent it arises from or relates to the Services, PHI that Practice
  or its patients give the Services, Practice's covered-entity status,
  instructions Practice gave, Practice's own breach of this agreement or
  violation of HIPAA or of state consumer-health-privacy law, or a claim by a
  patient, a regulator or any other third party. This indemnity applies to the
  fullest extent the law allows, including where the claim alleges negligence
  on healdesk's part, and the limits below do not apply to it.

  healdesk gives Practice no indemnity. To the fullest extent the law allows,
  healdesk is not liable to Practice for any loss arising out of or relating
  to this agreement or the Services, in contract, tort or otherwise, including
  lost profits and indirect, incidental, special or consequential damages.
  Where the law does not permit that exclusion, healdesk's total aggregate
  liability to Practice, across all claims, is limited to the Fees paid or
  payable by Practice in the twelve months before the event giving rise to the
  claim.

  This clause allocates cost between the parties. It does not change what each
  party owes a regulator directly under HIPAA, and Practice's own duty under
  45 CFR 164.404 to notify its patients remains Practice's.

  healdesk does not carry liability insurance, and its own suppliers cap their
  liability to healdesk at figures far below what a breach can cost. Practice
  should size its own cover accordingly.

18. AVAILABILITY OF RECORDS

  healdesk will make its internal practices, books and records relating to the
  use and disclosure of protected health information available to the
  Secretary of Health and Human Services for the purpose of determining
  compliance.

19. NOTICES, GOVERNING LAW, AND INTERPRETATION

  Notices under this agreement are in writing. To healdesk: [REGISTERED

  legal@healdesk.io. To Practice: the email address of the signatory below,
  and any notice address Practice later gives healdesk in writing. Breach
  reports may additionally be given by telephone to Practice, but the written

  This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware and by

  a regulation means that section as amended, and if a change in law requires
  a change to this agreement, the parties will agree it in good faith within
  thirty days of either asking, failing which either may terminate under the
  Term clause. If any provision is unenforceable the rest stands.

  This agreement, the Terms and the order Practice placed are the entire

  earlier version of this agreement between them from the effective date
