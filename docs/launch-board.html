15 sample reviews and an invented 4.9 rating from 286 reviews have shown as real since the "examples" label came off on 12 October; no real review is published. The FTC's 2024 rule on fake reviews makes this a legal risk.

Recommended: Claude removes the section Monday (C2); it returns with real reviews.

Your 30 Oct agreement release never reached main

BAA 1.7.0 and a DPA with 10-day breach and 15-day vendor notice were built on 30 Oct on a branch with no pull request. Checkout still offers BAA 1.6.0 and DPA 1.1.0 (60-day breach notice), and main's live DPA 1.1.0 is a different release, so the branch's numbers now collide.

Recommended: land it before launch so the first clinics sign the terms you chose. Claude reconciles it Monday (C13), you approve Tuesday.
D7

A second voice task before real calls?

One voice task runs today. If it fails mid-call, its calls drop, and new calls fail until AWS replaces it unless someone turns on Retell's outage mode by hand. A second task costs about $29 a month.

Recommended: yes, in the same infrastructure change as C5.
D8

Does your addendum release close its promise?

The promise says a clinic can elect direct booking under its agreement. Your 28 Oct release gives a clinic nothing to sign, and no page lets one elect it: booking follows each clinic's calendar terms.

Recommended: if that is what you meant, say yes and Claude records it On (C1); if not, Claude rewords the promise to what the product does.
