# Jobs to switch on for the pilot launch

This lists every background job on the console's Jobs page, System › Jobs (/admin/system/jobs), and what to do with each before the pilot opens on Thursday 5 November 2026. Claude wrote it on 1 November 2026 from the code, not from production: what is on or off right now shows only in the console. Every job ships switched off except the six marked "ships on", and none of them sends anything to a patient. Switch the first group on before your own test purchase (O14); O and C numbers are the launch board's. One job that go-live needs, `practice-questions`, cannot work until a fix to the host is deployed: see its row.

| Job (as the console names it) | What it does, in plain words | For launch | Why, and what it needs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `billing-notices` | The email sender. Every minute it sends the clinic emails waiting in the outbox: urgent and booking alerts, setup reminders, summaries and billing emails. | ON before go-live | Off, none of those go, and AYA's urgent reply leaves out "I've told the team". With the Resend key on the host (/admin/map shows whether it is there), this is what the go-live promise `alert-sender-on` asks for. Anything already waiting goes within a minute, so look at "Emails waiting to send" on /admin/system first. The welcome email, sign-in links and transcripts do not wait on this switch. |
| `practice-questions` | Runs AYA through practice questions two minutes after each setup change. A pass is what lets a channel go live; there is no approve button. | ON before go-live, after the fix in this row | Needs a fix first. It runs in the background worker, and the host did not give the worker the Anthropic key (`worker_secret_names` in infra/aws/locals.tf, and its test). The fix is on the branch (C5's second commit, 1 November 2026); until it is applied and deployed, every run ends "model unreachable", you get a `practice_question_failed` alert, and no channel can pass. /admin/map cannot show this gap: it checks the web side. Switch this job on after that apply and deploy. Each run spends a little on Anthropic. |
| `calendar-watch` | Every five minutes, checks that each connected Google Calendar still works. A broken one moves that location to booking requests at once, and the clinic and you are told. It also retries bookings Google never confirmed. | ON before go-live | Pilot clinics connect a calendar during setup, and direct booking is on. Switch it on before your demo-clinic booking test (O8). Needs the Google sign-in keys on the host, which the launch board says went in on 31 October. |
| `daily-summary` | The clinic's morning email at its opening time on working days: bookings, requests and what is still open. The week's first one carries last week's recap. | ON before go-live | With `weekly-recap`, this is what the go-live promise `daily-summary` asks for. Goes only to live clinics whose Daily summary setting is on, which is the default. Needs `billing-notices`. |
| `weekly-recap` | Last week's recap on Monday morning, for a live clinic that gets no daily summary (its setting is off, or it has no opening hours on file). | ON before go-live | Named in the same go-live promise. It never sends a week with no conversations. Needs `billing-notices`. |
| `requests-still-open` | A morning email counting booking requests still waiting for the front desk, for a clinic that switched its daily summary off. | ON before go-live | Without it, such a clinic hears of a request only when it comes in and when it expires. Needs `billing-notices`. |
| `setup-reminders` | Emails a clinic in setup every five days (days 5 to 30) with its next step, and once, 48 hours after its first channel goes live, about any channel still waiting. | ON before go-live | The Terms promise a reminder every five days. Needs `billing-notices`. Two cautions. The last reminder says the account closes at the end of day 30, which happens only while `setup-closure` is on (below). And a clinic that bought phone can be asked to set up call forwarding before real calls are on (O13); its callers are then passed to its direct line. |
| `usage-alerts` | Emails a clinic at 90% and at 100% of its monthly minutes or conversations, and tells you too. | ON before go-live | The Terms and the pricing page promise the 90% email. Only after a clinic's plan has started. Needs `billing-notices`. |
| `billing-requests` | Carries out billing changes on Stripe: the full monthly price from the day AYA first answers, a channel's price when it joins, and cancellations a clinic is owed. | ON before go-live | Off, a pilot clinic is never charged its monthly price, and a cancellation it is owed waits while billing goes on. Needs Stripe's live setup (O1); /admin/map shows whether the host's Stripe key is live or test. |
| `billing-reconcile` | Hourly safety net for billing: records any billing change that was missed, gives a week's notice before a channel that waits on the clinic joins the price, and finishes half-done terminations. | ON before go-live | Needs O1. Caution with phone: a phone line that is ready on our side but not live counts as waiting on the clinic, even while real calls are off, so about 30 days after it was ready it joins the clinic's price, after a week's notice email. Turn real calls on (O13) before a pilot clinic gets there. |
| `billing-sweep` | Hourly: suspends a clinic whose payment failed once its grace period ends, warning it a day before. For yearly plans it starts each month's allowance afresh. | ON before go-live | When you set Stripe's failed-payment rule to never cancel (docs/handover.md, Stripe dashboard settings), this job becomes the only thing that acts on a failed payment, so switch it on the same day. Nothing to do until a payment fails. |
| `usage-meter-report` | Every minute, tells Stripe about usage above the allowance on monthly plans, so the overage is on the next invoice. | ON before go-live | The Terms say overage is billed. Off, it bills zero, and usage reported after an invoice closes is lost for good. Needs O1. |
| `annual-overage-invoice` | Daily at 05 UTC: bills a yearly plan's overage once each month closes. | ON before go-live | The same Terms promise, for yearly plans. Nothing to do until a yearly pilot clinic finishes its first month. It charges the clinic's card: leave it off only if you decide pilot overage is free. Needs O1 and `billing-sweep`. |
| `card-expiring` | Daily at 07 UTC: emails a clinic whose card runs out before a renewal due within 30 days. | ON before go-live | Stripe sends no such warning for cards saved at checkout. Needs O1 and `billing-notices`. |
| `annual-renewal-reminder` | Daily at 09 UTC: emails a yearly-plan clinic 30 and 21 days before it renews. | ON before go-live | The Terms promise these notices, and nothing else sends them. Nothing to send for about 11 months. Needs `billing-notices`. |
| `billing-housekeeping` | Daily at 06 UTC: deletes short-lived records, such as unpaid checkout records, old message markers and chat threads idle for 30 days. | ON before go-live | Privacy clean-up: some of these records can identify a patient. Sends nothing. |
| `retention-purge` | At 03 and 09 UTC: deletes records past each clinic's retention period. | ON before go-live | It already runs for clinics on current agreements, switch or not; on adds every other clinic (older agreements, test clinics). docs/handover.md asks for it on since BAA 1.3.0. Deletes only what is due, and sends nothing. |
| `offboarding-destroy` | Hourly: deletes a departed clinic's patient data on its agreement's schedule, then closes its account. | ON before go-live | Like `retention-purge`: it already runs for current agreements; on adds clinics on older agreements or none, such as old test clinics. A deletion cannot be undone, and it only takes data that is due. |
| `lifecycle-watch` | Hourly: follows each paying clinic's setup and tells you when one stalls, nears its 30-day limit or is waiting on us. Sends nothing to clinics. | ON before go-live | Routine notes go to Slack only; urgent ones are emailed to you too, and decisions due show on Home. Without Slack, check each pilot clinic's Setup tab. Mark your own test clinic "Do not watch this account" on its Setup tab so it is not chased. |
| `health-flags` | Hourly: tells you when a clinic has had no conversation 48 hours after going live, or a live phone line no call for 14 days. | ON before go-live | Catches a broken install early. You only; the clinic never sees it. |
| `approvals-propose` | Hourly: puts billing failures that nothing else will retry into your approval list (Queues › Approvals) for a yes or no. It runs nothing itself. | ON before go-live | Your safety net for stuck money. Approving takes your passcode or authenticator code. |
| `clinic-audits` | Two minutes after a clinic changes its hours, closed days or team, checks that its setup still works. Results show on its Setup tab. | ON before go-live | Read-only, you only. While it is off, the Setup tab says the audits are off. |
| `clinic-audits-hourly` | The same checks every hour when something changed unrecorded, and every clinic's full set daily at 04 UTC. | ON before go-live | Read-only, you only. |
| `rights-watch` | Daily at 08 UTC: tells you about patients' requests (deletion, a copy, a correction) that are due within two business days, past due, or cannot run yet. | ON before go-live | These have legal deadlines. Sends nothing to a clinic or a patient. |
| `support-send` | Retries a support reply you approved when its first send failed, up to six tries, then tells you. | ON before go-live | Pilot clinics will use the support chat, and you can type and approve replies on each case's page. | `compliance-pack` | Each Monday from 07:00 New York: stores last week's compliance record and emails you a pointer with five numbers. | ON before go-live | Your weekly record once real patient data flows. The first run fills in up to five past weeks, so expect up to five emails at once. |
| `dmarc-review` | One reminder, ever, 14 days after healdesk first sent email: move healdesk.io's anti-spoofing record (DMARC) from "none" to "quarantine". | ON before go-live | You asked for this reminder on 6 October. Email has been going out for a while, so it may arrive at the next 08 UTC run, then never again. You only. |
| `ops-watch` | Tells you when a paid purchase has no account ten minutes later, when a promised follow-up or support reply is past its day, and when a vendor's agreement nears its end. | ON before go-live (ships on: check it still is) | The first sign that a pilot purchase went wrong. You only. |
| `ops-digest` | Your daily email from 07:00 UTC: queues, email health and connections, in ids and counts. | ON before go-live (ships on: check it still is) | Your morning check through the pilot. It goes to the ops address through Resend. |
| `incident-deadlines` | Hourly: tells you about an open incident past our 24-hour notice target, and puts first reports in your approval list. | ON before go-live (ships on: check it still is) | You only. | `transcript-emails` | Every minute: emails each conversation's transcript to the clinic and retries failures. They go through Amazon SES: check in the SES console that the account is out of the "sandbox", or SES delivers only to addresses verified in AWS. No record in the repo says this was done. |
| `site-reads` | Reads a clinic's website when we have no copy of it yet or the clinic corrects its address, to fill in setup and Knowledge. Mails nothing. | ON before go-live (ships on: check it still is) | Reads nothing until the host has the website-read address (`HEALDESK_WEB_URL`), which is part of C5. Until then its note says "HEALDESK_WEB_URL is not set". |
| `heartbeat` | Not a job: the worker's pulse, written every hour. | ON before go-live (ships on) | Its switch changes nothing. /api/health reports its age. |
| `setup-closure` | A switch, not a timer: while it is on, an account whose 30 days end with nothing live is cancelled and closed. Not on this page: the worker also runs about twenty small steps that have no switch, such as the welcome email's retry, booking requests expiring, the deletions the agreements promise, channel go-live checks and the Slack copies of alerts. They always run, and there is nothing to set.

## Check after switching

- **System › Jobs** (/admin/system/jobs): each row's Last run and Result. A job that is off still shows a fresh time with "disabled", so look for "ok". Every-minute jobs show "ok" within a few minutes (`calendar-watch` within five), hourly ones after the next full hour, and daily ones at their hour in UTC: 03 and 09 `retention-purge`, 05 `annual-overage-invoice`, 06 `billing-housekeeping`, 07 `card-expiring` and `ops-digest`, 08 `dmarc-review` and `rights-watch`, 09 `annual-renewal-reminder`; `compliance-pack` on Monday. "failed" twice in a row: read its note and ask Claude.
- **/api/health** (https://app.healdesk.io/api/health): `"ok": true`, and `heartbeatAgeSeconds` under about 3,700, since the worker ticks every hour. Over 7,200 means the worker is down. A 503 naming `critical_notice_undelivered` means an urgent clinic email is more than ten minutes late: check `billing-notices` and the Resend key.
- **Emails**: on System (/admin/system), "Emails waiting to send" should empty within minutes of switching `billing-notices` on. Each clinic's Deliveries tab shows every email to it and its state.
- **Ops alerts**: they come by email to the ops address, and to Slack once it is set up; Home (/admin) lists what needs you. Expect `compliance-pack`'s first emails and perhaps the DMARC reminder. A `practice_question_failed` alert means the worker still lacks the Anthropic key.
- **The 07:00 UTC digest** should arrive every morning, with each queue's oldest item.
- **`site-reads`**: its note stops saying "HEALDESK_WEB_URL is not set" once C5 is applied and deployed.
