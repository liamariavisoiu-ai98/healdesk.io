# Client notice audit, 29 October 2026

Docs only. No template, snapshot or code was changed. Audited at `origin/main` against the owner's standard of 29 October 2026, rules R1 to R6:

- R1 one plain sentence saying what happened, owned ("on our side", no blaming suppliers).
- R2 say what is protected, only if the code knows it.
- R3 one thing to do behind one button, or "you need do nothing".
- R4 say what we are doing; no timed follow-up we have no workflow for.
- R5 no jargon, passive voice, softeners; at most one short sincere ownership sentence.
- R6 always true; no PHI; the clinic should feel it is our top priority.

A search of `lib/email`, `lib/notices`, `lib/billing` and `lib/support` finds no "unfortunately", "apologise" or "inconvenience", so the softeners are already clean. The problems are elsewhere.

## Top 10, in plain English (hurts retention most first)

1. **Nothing tells a clinic when AYA is down or missing calls.** `outage_started`, `outage_ended` and `guarantee_miss` are still in `PENDING_TEMPLATES`, and `ROUTED_ANSWERED_COUNTER_BUILT = false`. If voice, chat or Meta fails, the only alert goes to our ops (for example `voice_call_start_failed`). The clinic learns from an angry patient. This is the largest retention risk, and the same unbuilt counter holds every go-live (`gate_b_not_built`).
2. **Payment failed, grace ending and suspended all say "forwarded calls pass straight through to your direct line, and website chat becomes a contact form"** (`WHEN_AYA_STOPS`). That is jargon. It leaves out Instagram, Messenger and WhatsApp, and never says in plain words that patients can still reach you. This is the highest-stakes money email, and it must read as "we are on it", not as a threat.
3. **The setup-closed email is cold and final.** "The account has closed... the setup fee is not refunded, as the terms say." It has no button, and the only way back is a reply. A clinic that stalled during setup is the easiest one to win back.
4. **Several dead-end "Reply to this email" instructions** (suspended for a non-payment cause, suspended-for-cause, `ai_paused` "if nobody switched it off") promise help that depends on the support drafter and an operator. That is a human queue, not a button. Add one Contact us button and name the reply-by day.
5. **Usage and overage emails talk about our costs** ("provisional until we've measured what each minute and conversation costs us"). That sounds like we are guessing at their bill. CLAUDE.md requires copy to say the rates are provisional, so this needs an owner decision on shorter wording (rows 9 and 12).
6. **Calendar stopped blames the clinic** ("usually stops when a Google password changes or someone removes healdesk's access"). Other reasons bookings flip to requests (`calendar_degraded`, unconfirmed time zone, unknown opening hours) send no email at all.
7. **The support drafter is told to "end with the next step and the day it happens"** (`lib/support/draft/prompt.ts`), and the reply fixture says "we'll call you on Monday". That is a timed follow-up with no scheduling workflow behind it (R4). Tie any date to the reply-by day only.
8. **Setup reminders 1 to 5 quote the account-closing penalty in every email.** Five "you will lose your fee" mails read as a threat. The last-day mail is fine; the first five should lead with help.
9. **The first-period refund promise** ("We'll email you by {day} to arrange the refund") is backed only by an ops alert (`first_period_refund_due`, raised in `setAutoRenew`). The day is a convention, not a timer. Keep the promise, and make ops treat the date as a hard SLA.
10. **Portal states use internal vocabulary:** "healdesk hasn't switched on live booking yet" (`kill_switch_off`), "Your assistant's script hasn't been approved yet", "Calendar connection isn't available yet". They tell the clinic nothing it can act on and sound unfinished.

## Notes for whoever implements this

- Do not send any rewrite without the owner reading it. The account-closing and refund lines are legal-adjacent.
- Rows 1, 4 and 5 share `WHEN_AYA_STOPS` and `undoForwarding` in `billing-copy.ts`. Fix them once there.
- Any "callers reach your direct line" sentence must use `fallbackLine`, so it is never printed for a clinic with no direct line set.
- Snapshots change when templates change. Regenerate them in the same commit.
- Rows 9 and 12 conflict with CLAUDE.md's "Copy says so" about provisional overage rates. The owner decides.
