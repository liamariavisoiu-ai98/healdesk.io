## Left unchanged on purpose

- The fee-not-refunded line in setup_closed stays. The Terms say the setup fee is never refunded, and the clinic should read that once, at closure.
- The "Reply to this email" lines in the non-paying-cause suspended and suspended_for_cause emails (audit finding 4) are out of scope. They need a Contact us route.
- No bought-channel list exists in these payloads, so the block cannot be channel-exact. Adding it would change the payload types.
- setup_closed has no "Resume setup" button: the account is closed, and the wizard is not reachable. /help/call needs no sign-in, shows the scheduling page when one is set, and otherwise says how to reach us. apps/app has no support-form page, and mailto: links fail the email lint (https only).
