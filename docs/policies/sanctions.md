# Sanction policy — §164.308(a)(1)(ii)(C)

**Effective:** 13 September 2026.

Anyone who works for or with healdesk and does not follow the
[access-control policy](./access-control.md), the "never log PHI" rule, or the
Business Associate Agreement's terms is subject to sanction. This applies to the
founder: the sanction for the founder is documented in the training log, reported
to the affected clinic where the BAA requires it, and, where the act is a
reportable disclosure, reported to the regulator on the same clock as anyone else's.

| Severity | Examples | Sanction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Careless | A PHI-shaped log line caught in review; a laptop without disk encryption | Documented warning; re-training; the fix merged before any other work |
| Serious | Reading a patient record without a business reason; exporting PHI to a laptop | Access suspended immediately pending review; written finding; for a contractor, termination |
| Wilful or repeated | Sharing PHI outside the BAA's permitted uses; disabling a control | Termination; breach process; notification to the clinic and, where required, to HHS and the individual |

Every sanction is recorded in the [training log](./training-log.md) under
"Sanctions", with the date, the finding and the action.
