03Checkout

six steps · signing is step 5 · paying is step 6
  1. STEP 1specialtyEight, from med spa to veterinary. Prefilled from wherever they came from; still shown.
  2. STEP 2platformBoulevard · GoHighLevel · Zenoti · PatientNow · Acuity · Vagaro · Mindbody · none.
  3. STEP 3attributionEstimated from their own average treatment value, or verified from settled sales.
  4. STEP 4detailsIncluding the phone system: landline, VoIP, or the honest “unknown”.
  5. STEP 5sign the BAAIts own screen. Scroll to the end, answer the covered-entity question, type your name. Written to the database here.
  6. STEP 6plan & payCore $249 · Growth $449 · Multi $799. No setup fee. 14-day trial. Carries only the id of what was already signed.

Why that order

contract before money

rule

A healthcare provider may not hand patient information to a supplier without a written contract first. So it cannot be a checkbox bundled into the payment button, and it cannot be something a webhook records afterwards. The visible consequence: abandoning at step 6 leaves a signed agreement and no charge — and that is the correct outcome.

Deliberately absent

integration

web

Connecting a booking platform is not on the critical path to a purchase. Nothing about the sale waits on a clinic finding its API key, which is the step most likely to stall for three weeks. The link for it is issued later, once.

the boundary

One signed request, and nothing else

apps/web has no Drizzle, no pg, no connection string — and must stay that way. That single fact is what makes the split enforceable rather than aspirational. When the marketing site needs something from the PHI side, it asks, and the PHI side decides what to answer.

Hop 1 · stripe

checkout.session.completed

Not the success URL. A buyer who closes the tab after paying still gets their account; a stranger who hand-crafts a success URL gets nothing.
Hop 2 · apps/web

verify raw body → HMAC re-sign

The Stripe secret lives here, the database lives there. Re-serialising the body would change the bytes and fail the signature, so the raw text is what gets verified.
Hop 3 · apps/app

POST /api/provision

Signature checked before the body is even parsed as JSON, so an unauthenticated caller cannot make us do work.
Retry law

every non-2xx, for 3 days

So a failure a later attempt could fix must be non-2xx, and a failure no attempt will ever fix must be 2xx plus a loud noise — or the endpoint gets disabled and every later sale is lost.

07Prove it

this is the product; everything else feeds it honest inputs

Rule 1

a deposit is not attendance

app

Money taken at booking proves the appointment was made, nothing more. Treating it as evidence of a visit counts every forfeited deposit as a successful one — inflating precisely the number we are asking to be trusted on.

Rule 2

ambiguity is not a guess

exception

Two appointments in a week and one payment produces an exception, not a coin flip. A wrong match is worse than an unmatched one: the unmatched is a caveat the clinic can check, the wrong one silently misattributes revenue. Confidence is only ever exact, high or medium.

Rule 3

unmatched money is surfaced

app

Sales we cannot place go to the exception queue with an amount attached, and the total appears in the report as a caveat. A total that quietly omits what it could not explain is the failure mode this whole system exists to replace.
