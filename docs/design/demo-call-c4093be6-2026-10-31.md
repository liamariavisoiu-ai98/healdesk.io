# Demo call c4093be6: diagnosis, fixes and the line-by-line replay

Task DEMO-CALL-C409. Owner, 31 October 2026, with the transcript of demo call
c4093be6 (an owner, Josh, calling about Miami Skin & Vein) and the prompt
`aya-voice-debug-prompt.md`: diagnose with file and line evidence, plan, and
after the go-ahead ("go ahead with all the fixes") fix and replay every caller
line against nine acceptance criteria.

## What went wrong, in three causes

1. **The role-play ended on her judgement, and the call then sold to a
   patient.**
   - The role-play note told her to wrap up as soon as a request was taken
     (`demo-facts.ts`, `roleplayNote`). She ended the first role-play while
     the "patient" was moving their booking.
   - "I want to do the role play again", said inside the next role-play, came
     back as a `cancel`. The call went back to the pitch and added the exit
     line (`retell.ts`, the stage block).
   - Her own streamed "Ring ring!" plus the opener the call appends made two
     greetings.
   - From then on the owner kept playing the patient. Every turn after that
     ran on healdesk's sales prompt and notes: the close and the 50% discount
     on every reply, `fastLane` recommending a package, hello@ from the
     `noCall` line, and "our side".
2. **Refused answers were replaced by filler.**
   - After the role-play, a reply the guard refused was asked again, then
     replaced by the owner-mode recovery line ("Sorry, I lost my train of
     thought", "Let me put that another way") or, midway, by its bridge
     ("Anyway, where were we?") (`turn.ts` `softRefusal`, `demo-facts.ts`
     `demoRecovery`).
   - The guard's own line names no provider.
   - The sentence splitter cut "Dr. Patel" after "Dr." (`turn.ts`
     `SpokenStream`), so a refusal of the next piece left "that's a question
     for Dr. Anyway, where were we?".
3. **The booking was the model's to remember.** Nothing in code checked:
   - the hours ("I'll take Thursday at nine PM, though our hours don't run
     that late");
   - free times ("Thursday afternoon works");
   - a name and a number before "I'll pass your request to the team";
   - that a cancelled request no longer stood ("when they confirm your Friday
     morning request").

Also found:

- **Urgent line:** the demo's urgent reply named no number. A demo has no
  clinic number on its facts, so it fell back to 9-1-1 alone.
- **"47 hours":** a rebuild of the demo set `readyAt` afresh, so the offer's
  48 hours started again (`apps/web/src/lib/demo/build.ts`).
- **Keyword list:** the demo engine agent has none (clinic agents have their
  menu's).
- **Interruption sensitivity:** already 0.8 for every agent
  (`RETELL_CALL_SETTINGS`). The cut-off replies came from reply length, not
  the setting.
- **"Fellowship-trained, every treatment by him":** that is on the clinic's
  site, so it was not invented. Ranking providers was not forbidden anywhere.
- **Prices:** $80 a month per 100 minutes is the catalog's Aya Voice step, and
  the 50% is a real per-demo offer (the demo built and sent by an operator),
  counted from `readyAt`.

## Decisions taken by default, for the owner to confirm

- **Urgent wording:** unchanged. It is the catalog's fixed reply, which waits
  on the medical director's sign-off. The demo now adds the clinic's published
  number to it, as a patient's call adds the clinic's urgent-help number.
  Nothing is flagged from a demo, because a demo sends nothing. A patient's
  call raises the urgent alert and says "I've told the team."
- **Calendar:** the synthetic calendar made from the clinic's hours stays
  (`demoSlots`). The call now offers only its times, or two times on the day
  the caller names. A sandbox Google Calendar for the demo is the owner's
  decision.
- **Per-unit question:** the catalog's clinical screen, which the Meta
  channels share, refuses any sentence that says what a unit is. Aya now says
  "The price is per unit, and the total depends on the area, which Dr. Shaun
  Patel works out with you at a consultation". Explaining what a unit is
  would widen the clinical screen, so that is the owner's decision.

- **Provider-named guard lines for every clinic:** a clinic's own calls and
  chats still say "That's a question for the clinicians at <clinic>...".
  Naming the provider there too changes the fixed safety lines that the
  safety suite pins, on every channel. That is the owner's decision, and it
  is one line in `turn.ts` (`routedTo`).

## S10: the transcript email (report only, nothing changed)

- **What triggers it:**
  - A demo call started by an operator signed in to `/admin` is sealed as a
    test (`context.test`).
  - When the call ends, `retell.ts` `emailDemoTranscript` renders it with the
    clinic transcript template (`renderTranscriptEmail`). That template is why
    it says it holds patient information.
  - It is sent to hello@ (`SENDER_IDENTITY.replyTo`). A prospect's demo call
    sends nothing.
- **What it holds:** the call's own turns as the session kept them, in memory
  only.
- **What is stored:**
  - Nothing is queued or kept in apps/app, unlike a clinic's transcript,
    which waits sealed in the outbox until SES reports delivery.
  - Retell's agent keeps basic attributes only, for one day.
  - Retell's PII redaction is not configured on the agent, and it would not
    apply anyway: the email is built from apps/app's own turns, not Retell's
    transcript.
- **Transport:** `sendTranscriptEmail` with the `may-carry-phi` class goes
  through SES with TLS required, never Resend. Without an SES configuration
  it is not sent at all.
- **Decision:** the owner's, with counsel.
