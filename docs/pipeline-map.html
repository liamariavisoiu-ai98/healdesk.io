healdesk healdesk.io · the whole motion, one page

The machine sells itself.
Switching it on is still by hand.

Thirty-three steps carry a stranger to a clinic whose recovered revenue we can prove. A buyer can go from cold email to paid without a person touching anything — but everything after the payment either needs one of us, or has not been wired up yet. Read out of the code, not the plan.

16 ● automatic

Runs with nobody watching. The whole path to a paid account is here.

6 ◑ by hand

A person acts — five of them us, one of them the clinic, once.

5 ◔ built, switched off

The code exists and is tested. Nothing calls it yet.

6 ○ not built

Three on purpose, three still owed. Named, never implied.

Put plainly: selling is automatic, onboarding is manual, and proving it is written but unplugged. The gap is not in the hard part — the matcher that ties money to visits is finished and tested — it is in the boring wiring: no mail transport, no timer, no real connector.

◑ every human touch in the motion

Six hands, and one of them isn't ours

Nothing between the cold email and the cleared payment needs a person. After it, six things do — and the customer's share of all six is a single paste, once, that they never repeat.

Us · five touches

  1. Sweep a new metroRun the leadgen pipeline per city; it hands back a scored CSV.
  2. Read and publish a reviewSo the first person here to see a testimonial isn't seeing it on the homepage.
  3. Issue the connect linkOne click in /admin after a clinic pays.
  4. Send that linkOnly until a mail transport is configured — then it goes on its own.
  5. Switch a job onDeliberate, named, and recorded. This one stays manual.

The clinic · one touch, ever

  1. Paste one read-only credentialThey click a link in an email, follow their platform's wizard, paste one key, and see a read-back of exactly what we can and cannot see. Then they are finished.

No account, no password, no session, no console. A clinic-facing tool was built once and deleted. healdesk is a service, not software a clinic operates — so the link is the credential.
