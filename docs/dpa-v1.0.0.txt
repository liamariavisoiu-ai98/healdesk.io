HEALDESK DATA PROTECTION AGREEMENT
Version 1.0.0 — executed electronically

This Agreement takes effect on the date below. It governs Client Information,
as defined below, that healdesk creates, receives, maintains or transmits for
Practice in the course of the Services defined in the Terms. HIPAA does not
apply to Client Information, for the reason the clause on Practice's status
gives; healdesk protects it under this agreement with the safeguards it uses
for protected health information.

BETWEEN
  Glow Med Spa LLC ("Practice"), a medical spa, dermatology or aesthetics
  practice
  Viixi FZC, a company registered under the Sharjah Publishing City Free Zone,
  United Arab Emirates ("healdesk"), of 800 N King Street, Suite 304-1426,
  Wilmington, DE 19801, United States, and by email to legal@healdesk.io

SIGNED FOR PRACTICE BY
  Alex Chen, Owner
  alex@glowmedspa.test

EFFECTIVE 6 October 2026

1. DEFINITIONS

  "Practice" means Glow Med Spa LLC. "healdesk" means Viixi FZC, a company
  registered under the Sharjah Publishing City Free Zone, United Arab
  Emirates. "Terms" means the healdesk Terms of Service at healdesk.io/terms
  as in force on the effective date. "Services" and "Fees" have the meanings
  the Terms give them.

  A "client" is a person who contacts Practice through the Services, or about
  whom Practice gives healdesk information, in the course of seeking or
  receiving Practice's services. "Client Information" means any information
  about a client that healdesk creates, receives, maintains or transmits for
  Practice in the course of the Services, including what a client says in a
  call or message.

  "HIPAA" means the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of
  1996 and its regulations at 45 CFR Parts 160 and 164, and "covered entity"
  and "protected health information" have the meanings those regulations give
  them. A "security incident" is the attempted or successful unauthorised
  access to, or use, disclosure, modification or destruction of, Client
  Information, or interference with the systems that hold it. A "breach" is
  the unauthorised acquisition of, or access to, Client Information that
  compromises its security, confidentiality or integrity. "Business day" means
  a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or United States federal holiday.

  This agreement governs Client Information. Where this agreement and the
  Terms conflict about Client Information, this agreement governs.

2. PRACTICE'S STATUS

  Practice has told healdesk that it does not transmit health information
  electronically in connection with any transaction covered by HIPAA: it sends
  no claim, eligibility check, prior authorisation or claim-status enquiry
  electronically to any health plan, itself or through anyone acting for it.
  Practice warrants that this is true on the effective date. On that basis
  Practice is not a covered entity, the Client Information healdesk handles
  for it is not protected health information, and this agreement is not a
  business associate agreement.

  healdesk nonetheless protects Client Information under this agreement with
  the safeguards it uses for protected health information. State privacy,
  consumer-health and breach-notification laws may apply to Client Information
  and to both parties, and in some states carry a private right of action.

  Practice will tell healdesk in writing before it first sends any such
  transaction, and as soon as it learns that it is a covered entity for any
  other reason. From that notice, healdesk will pause the assistant for
  Practice until Practice signs healdesk's business associate agreement, which
  then replaces this agreement from that agreement's effective date. While the
  assistant is paused, calls Practice forwards to healdesk pass straight
  through to Practice's direct line, as the Terms provide.

3. PRACTICE'S INSTRUCTIONS

  Practice decides what the Services do with Client Information, through the
  Terms and the settings Practice chooses in its portal, which are its
  instructions to healdesk. healdesk will tell Practice if it believes an
  instruction would break a law that applies to Client Information, and need
  not follow that instruction.

  Practice will tell healdesk, promptly and in writing, of any limit it has
  agreed with a client, and of any permission a client has withdrawn, that
  affects how healdesk may use or disclose that client's information. Practice
  will not ask healdesk to use or disclose Client Information in a way the law
  would not permit Practice itself to, and is responsible for any notice the
  law requires Practice to give its clients, and any consent it requires
  Practice to obtain from them, for their information to be handled through
  the Services.

4. WHAT HEALDESK MAY DO WITH CLIENT INFORMATION

  healdesk may use and disclose Client Information only as necessary to
  provide the Services to Practice, to keep them secure, and as required by
  law. It may not use or disclose it in any way that Practice could not.

5. WHERE PRACTICE IS NOT A COVERED ENTITY

  healdesk processes Client Information only on Practice's documented
  instructions, which are the Terms and Practice's portal settings, and for no
  other purpose. It does not sell or share it, does not retain, use or
  disclose it outside the direct business relationship with Practice or for
  any purpose other than providing the Services, and does not combine it with
  information from any other source except as the Services require. healdesk
  will give Practice the information it needs to answer a consumer's access,
  deletion or correction request within ten business days; will tell Practice
  if it can no longer meet these obligations; will let Practice take
  reasonable steps to stop and remedy any unauthorised use; and will provide,
  on request, the documents Practice reasonably needs to verify compliance.

6. PAYMENTS

  Where Practice takes deposits, healdesk creates a payment link through
  Practice's own payment processor. The link and the payment carry an amount,
  a generic description and a reference number, and no service name or
  clinical detail, including in metadata and the statement descriptor.
  healdesk never takes card numbers.

7. WHAT HEALDESK READS FROM YOUR SYSTEMS

  healdesk holds no credentials to Mindbody, or to any other
  practice-management or point-of-sale system, and reads nothing from them.
  The only Client Information healdesk receives is what clients themselves
  give it during a call or chat.

8. PRACTICE'S GOOGLE CALENDAR

  Practice has told healdesk that its booking system, Mindbody, does not read
  a Google Calendar for conflicts, or has not been confirmed to. healdesk
  therefore does not connect to Practice's Google Calendar, reads nothing from
  it and writes nothing to it, and the assistant does not book appointments
  itself: it takes each booking as a request, which Practice's staff confirm
  in Practice's own system.

  If Practice changes its booking system, or wants the assistant to book into
  a Google Calendar, Practice first records a calendar election for that
  location.

  This clause governs the calendar of the location Practice sets up when it
  signs this agreement. For any other location, and for that location once
  Practice changes the kind of Google account it uses or its booking system,
  the calendar election Practice records for that location takes the place of
  this clause for that location.

9. SAFEGUARDS

  healdesk will use appropriate administrative, physical and technical
  safeguards to prevent any use or disclosure of Client Information this
  agreement does not permit. They are the safeguards it uses for protected
  health information: healdesk will apply Subpart C of 45 CFR Part 164, the
  HIPAA Security Rule, to Client Information as if it were electronic
  protected health information, although HIPAA does not itself apply to it.

  Client Information is held on United States infrastructure and encrypted at
  rest. In transit it is encrypted wherever healdesk controls both ends, and
  healdesk will not transmit Client Information over a channel that cannot
  negotiate encryption. An email whose receiving server will not negotiate
  encryption is failed, not sent, and healdesk tells Practice rather than send
  it in the clear; the list of clients in an incident report travels by secure
  transfer, never in the email itself.

  One narrow exception: a client who telephones has chosen the telephone, and
  a telephone call is not end-to-end encrypted. healdesk may answer such a
  call, limited to the minimum necessary and never including clinical detail,
  test results or payment information. healdesk places no calls and sends no
  SMS. This exception does not extend to any other channel or content.

10. MINIMUM NECESSARY, AND WHO RECEIVES CLIENT INFORMATION

  Each party will limit its use, disclosure and requests of Client Information
  to the minimum necessary to accomplish the purpose.

  The assistant asks a client for no more than Practice's front desk needs to
  place a booking: a name, a telephone number, the service asked about, the
  times that suit them, whether they have been to Practice before, whether the
  visit is a consultation or a treatment, and any practitioner they prefer. It
  does not ask for a history, a medication or a diagnosis, and it never takes
  a card number. On every channel its opening line says that it is an AI
  assistant and, on a call, that the call is transcribed; Practice cannot
  switch that statement off or edit it out. No call audio is kept at all.

  healdesk discloses Client Information only to: Practice itself; healdesk's
  subcontractors, under the Subcontractors clause; the systems Practice
  connects and controls, which are its calendar, as the Google Calendar clause
  describes, its payment processor, as the Payments clause describes, and the
  messaging accounts Practice connects with Meta, in a conversation a client
  started there; and anyone to whom the law requires healdesk to disclose it,
  after telling Practice where the law allows. It discloses Client Information
  to no one else.

11. SUBCONTRACTORS

  healdesk uses subcontractors to deliver the Services — telephony, speech
  recognition, language models, hosting and email among them. healdesk will
  ensure that any subcontractor that creates, receives, maintains or transmits
  Client Information on healdesk's behalf agrees in writing, before it does
  so, to restrictions and conditions at least as protective of Client
  Information as those this agreement places on healdesk. healdesk remains
  answerable to Practice for its subcontractors' acts and omissions with
  Client Information.

  Language models are used to understand a client and answer them during a
  conversation. healdesk's language-model provider is Anthropic, which
  receives the content of each conversation to generate the assistant's
  replies, in the same account healdesk uses for the practices HIPAA covers,
  under a business associate agreement with healdesk. In that account
  Anthropic retains conversation content for 30 days, for abuse detection and
  security, and then deletes it, except content its automated trust-and-safety
  systems flag or the law requires it to keep, which it may retain for up to
  two years; its covered service cannot run with shorter retention. Anthropic
  never trains on that content, and healdesk may not use Client Information to
  train, fine-tune or evaluate any model, its own or a third party's.

  healdesk keeps a current list of its subcontractors at
  healdesk.io/subprocessors and will give Practice at least 24 hours' notice
  by email before adding one that will handle the information this agreement
  protects. If Practice objects on reasonable grounds before the change takes
  effect, Practice may terminate this agreement without penalty.

12. REPORTING INCIDENTS AND BREACHES

  healdesk will report to Practice, in writing to the address in the Notices
  clause:

    a. any use or disclosure of Client Information not permitted by this
       agreement, within five business days of healdesk becoming aware of it;
    b. any breach, without unreasonable delay and in no case later than five
       calendar days after discovery. A breach is treated as discovered on the
       first day it is known, or by exercising reasonable diligence would have
       been known, to healdesk or to any person, other than the one committing
       it, who is healdesk's workforce member or agent. Five days is
       deliberately short, to leave Practice time to meet any duty it has
       under state breach-notification law to notify the people affected;
    c. any security incident, within five business days of becoming aware of
       it — except routine unsuccessful attempts that compromised nothing,
       such as blocked sign-ins, port scans and filtered traffic, which the
       parties agree this clause reports once, here, and which healdesk will
       summarise on request.

  A breach report will include, to the extent known and supplemented as
  learned: the identification of each client whose Client Information was or
  is reasonably believed to have been accessed, acquired, used or disclosed; a
  description of what happened, including the dates of the breach and of its
  discovery; the types of Client Information involved; what healdesk is doing
  to investigate, mitigate and prevent recurrence; and a contact. Who bears
  the cost of notifying clients, regulators and anyone else about a breach is
  allocated by the Liability clause.

13. CLIENTS' REQUESTS

  When a client asks Practice to see, correct or delete their Client
  Information, healdesk will give Practice what it needs to answer within ten
  business days of Practice asking, and will correct or delete that
  information as Practice directs. The telephone number of a client whose
  information is deleted is kept only as the record that the deletion was
  asked for.

  If a client makes such a request to healdesk directly, healdesk will not
  answer it. It will pass the request to Practice within five business days
  and tell the client it has done so.

14. CONFIDENTIALITY

  healdesk will keep Client Information confidential. It will let only the
  people who need Client Information to provide the Services see it, and will
  bind each of them to keep it confidential.

15. WHAT HEALDESK IS NOT AUTHORISED TO DO

  · Use Client Information to train, fine-tune, or evaluate any artificial
    intelligence model, its own or a third party's.
  · Disclose Client Information to any advertising or analytics platform, in
    any form, including hashed or aggregated. Instagram, Messenger and
    WhatsApp are operated by Meta, which is not healdesk's subcontractor and
    is not bound by this agreement; where Practice connects them, the
    assistant replies there only inside a conversation the client started,
    handles no clinical content, confirms no appointment details, and moves
    anything clinical to a call or to Practice's own secure route.
  · Provide clinical advice, diagnosis, triage, or treatment recommendations
    through the assistant.
  · Sell, license, or otherwise monetise Client Information.

16. ASSISTANCE AND AUDIT

  healdesk will give Practice the reasonable help it needs to meet its own
  obligations under the laws that apply to Client Information, including to
  answer a regulator or a client, and will answer Practice's reasonable
  written questions about how healdesk meets this agreement. healdesk will
  make its practices, books and records relating to Client Information
  available to a regulator with authority over Practice or over healdesk,
  where the law requires it to.

17. TERM, TERMINATION, AND WHAT HAPPENS TO THE INFORMATION

  This agreement takes effect on the effective date and continues for as long
  as healdesk holds any Client Information for Practice. Either party may
  terminate it on written notice if the other materially breaches it and does
  not cure the breach within thirty days of being told of it in writing; where
  cure is not possible, on notice. Practice may also terminate under the
  subcontractor-objection right above. Ending the Services ends this
  agreement, subject to the rest of this clause. A business associate
  agreement that replaces this agreement under the clause on Practice's status
  ends it too: the Client Information healdesk then holds is governed by that
  agreement from its effective date, and nothing is destroyed because of the
  replacement.

  When the Services end, the assistant stops answering for Practice, and
  healdesk uses Client Information only to pass forwarded calls through to
  Practice's direct line for as long as the Terms provide, to give Practice
  its export, and then to destroy it. Within thirty days after the Services
  end, healdesk will deliver to Practice an export of the Client Information
  it holds, in a usable electronic form. healdesk's destruction runs once a
  day. It destroys Practice's Client Information at the first run once thirty
  days have passed since the Services ended, if the export has been delivered
  by then; otherwise at the first run after the export is delivered; and in
  any case at the first run once ninety days have passed since the Services
  ended, whether or not the export has been delivered.

  Destruction deletes the conversations, bookings, booking requests and
  contact details healdesk holds for Practice, and the credentials Practice
  connected through healdesk. After it, healdesk holds no Client Information
  for Practice except the telephone number of any client who asked to have
  their information deleted, kept only as the record of that request. Copies
  in encrypted database backups are deleted as those backups expire. healdesk
  will also have its subcontractors delete what they hold for Practice, except
  what the Subcontractors clause says Anthropic may keep. What is in systems
  Practice controls, such as its calendar, is Practice's own.

  Where destroying any Client Information is not feasible, healdesk will say
  why in writing, keep protecting it under this agreement for as long as it
  holds it, and use it only for what makes destruction infeasible. The signed
  copy of this agreement is kept for at least six years after it ends.

  This clause allocates cost between the parties. It does not change what each

  healdesk does not carry liability insurance, and its own suppliers cap their
