Practice in the course of the Services defined in the Terms. HIPAA does not
apply to Client Information, for the reason the clause on Practice's status
gives; healdesk protects it under this agreement with the safeguards it uses
for protected health information.

1. DEFINITIONS

  "Practice" means Glow Med Spa LLC. "healdesk" means Viixi FZC, a company
  registered under the Sharjah Publishing City Free Zone, United Arab
  Emirates. "Terms" means the healdesk Terms of Service at healdesk.io/terms
  as in force on the effective date. "Services" and "Fees" have the meanings
  the Terms give them.

  A "client" is a person who contacts Practice through the Services, or about
  whom Practice gives healdesk information, in the course of seeking or
  receiving Practice's services. "Client Information" means any information
  about a client that healdesk creates, receives, maintains or transmits for
  Practice in the course of the Services, including what a client says in a
  call or message.

  "HIPAA" means the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of
  1996 and its regulations at 45 CFR Parts 160 and 164, and "covered entity"
  and "protected health information" have the meanings those regulations give
  them. A "security incident" is the attempted or successful unauthorised
  access to, or use, disclosure, modification or destruction of, Client
  Information, or interference with the systems that hold it. A "breach" is
  the unauthorised acquisition of, or access to, Client Information that
  compromises its security, confidentiality or integrity, and also includes
  anything an applicable state breach-notification law treats as a breach of
  the security of Client Information. "Calendar election" means the document,

  This agreement governs Client Information. Where this agreement and the
  Terms conflict about Client Information, or about terminating this agreement
  and what then happens to the Services and the Fees, this agreement governs.

  Practice warrants that this is true on the effective date. On that basis
  Practice is not a covered entity, the Client Information healdesk handles

  healdesk nonetheless protects Client Information under this agreement with
  the safeguards it uses for protected health information. State privacy,

9. SAFEGUARDS

  healdesk will use appropriate administrative, physical and technical
  safeguards to prevent any use or disclosure of Client Information this
  agreement does not permit. They are the safeguards it uses for protected
  health information: healdesk will apply Subpart C of 45 CFR Part 164, the
  HIPAA Security Rule, to Client Information as if it were electronic
  protected health information, although HIPAA does not itself apply to it.

  Client Information is held on United States infrastructure and encrypted at
  rest. In transit it is encrypted wherever healdesk controls both ends, and
  healdesk will not transmit Client Information over a channel that cannot
  negotiate encryption. An email whose receiving server will not negotiate
  encryption is failed, not sent, and healdesk tells Practice rather than send
  it in the clear; the list of clients in an incident report travels by secure
  transfer, never in the email itself.

  One narrow exception: a client who telephones has chosen the telephone, and
  a telephone call is not end-to-end encrypted. healdesk may answer such a
  call, limited to the minimum necessary and never including clinical detail,
  test results or payment information. healdesk places no calls and sends no
  SMS. This exception does not extend to any other channel or content.

10. MINIMUM NECESSARY, AND WHO RECEIVES CLIENT INFORMATION

  Each party will limit its use, disclosure and requests of Client Information
  to the minimum necessary to accomplish the purpose.

  The assistant asks a client for no more than Practice's front desk needs to
  place a booking: a name, a telephone number, the service asked about, the
  times that suit them, whether they have been to Practice before, whether the
  visit is a consultation or a treatment, and any practitioner they prefer. It
  does not ask for a history, a medication or a diagnosis, and it never takes
  a card number. On every channel its opening line says that it is an AI
  assistant and, on a call, that the call is transcribed; Practice cannot
  switch that statement off or edit it out. No call audio is kept at all.

  healdesk does not keep transcripts. When a call or a website chat ends,
  healdesk emails its transcript to the email address on Practice's healdesk
  account, and to no other address, only over an encrypted connection, as the
  Safeguards clause provides. So that an email that fails or is returned can
  be sent again, healdesk holds one encrypted copy of the transcript only
  until Practice's mail server confirms that it has received the email, and
  then deletes the copy at once. If a send fails or the email is returned,
  healdesk keeps the copy and sends the email again until it is delivered. If
  the email is accepted for delivery but neither its delivery nor its failure
  is confirmed within 72 hours of its last sending, healdesk deletes the copy
  then. In no case does healdesk hold the copy for more than 30 days after the
  transcript was queued for sending: a copy whose email has not been delivered
  by then is deleted. While it holds the copy, healdesk may also send the
  email again when Practice asks. healdesk keeps a record of each transcript's
  delivery, without what was said or written in it, for as long as it keeps
  the record of that conversation. It keeps no other copy of a transcript,
  apart from the backups described below; what Anthropic keeps is what the
  Subcontractors clause describes. Once healdesk's copy is deleted, the email
  is Practice's own record of what was said, and Practice is responsible for
  keeping, protecting and deleting it.

  healdesk keeps the record of each conversation, without what was said or
  written in it, for 90 days after it took place, and each appointment record
  for 90 days after the appointment's time. It keeps each booking request for
  90 days after the latest of when it was made, the time it was confirmed for
  and its appointment's time. A client's contact details are kept while any
  conversation, appointment record or booking request of theirs is kept, and
  in any case until 90 days after the last time those details were recorded or
  changed. A backup copy of anything healdesk deletes expires within 35 days
  of the deletion. The clause headed "Term, termination, and what happens to
  the information" governs what is held after the Services end.

  healdesk discloses Client Information only to: Practice itself; healdesk's
  subcontractors, under the Subcontractors clause; the systems Practice
  connects and controls, which are its calendar, as the Google Calendar clause
  or a calendar election describes, its payment processor, as the Payments
  clause describes, and the messaging accounts Practice connects with Meta, in
  a conversation a client started there; and anyone to whom the law requires
  healdesk to disclose it, after telling Practice where the law allows. It
  discloses Client Information to no one else.

12. REPORTING INCIDENTS AND BREACHES

  healdesk will notify Practice, by email to Practice's address in the Notices
  clause, of any of the following without unreasonable delay and in no case
  later than 60 calendar days after discovery:

    a. any use or disclosure of Client Information that this agreement does
       not permit, by healdesk or by a subcontractor;
    b. any breach;
    c. any security incident, including anything a state breach-notification
       law treats as a breach of the security of Client Information.

  Routine unsuccessful attempts that compromised nothing, such as blocked
  sign-ins, port scans and filtered traffic, are security incidents that the
  parties agree this clause reports once, here; healdesk will summarise them
  on request.

  A breach is treated as discovered on the first day healdesk knows of it, or
  by exercising reasonable diligence would have known of it. healdesk knows
  what any of its workforce members or agents, other than the person
  committing the breach, knows or by exercising reasonable diligence would
  have known. Anything else this clause covers is discovered when healdesk
  becomes aware of it.

  healdesk delays a notice only where a law enforcement official states that
  the notice would impede a criminal investigation or damage national
  security. It then delays it only for as long as the law that applies
  provides, and gives it as soon as that delay ends.

  Each notice is also a written report, sent to the same address and as
  complete as healdesk can then make it, and healdesk will add to the report
  promptly as information becomes available. For a breach, the report will
  include, to the extent possible, what Practice needs for its own notices:

  · the identification of each client whose Client Information has been, or is
    reasonably believed by healdesk to have been, accessed, acquired, used or
    disclosed during the breach, sent by secure transfer as the Safeguards
    clause provides;
  · a brief description of what happened, including the date of the breach and
    the date of its discovery;
  · the types of Client Information involved;
  · any steps clients should take to protect themselves from potential harm
    resulting from the breach;
  · what healdesk is doing to investigate the breach, to mitigate harm to
    clients and to protect against further breaches;
  · whether a law enforcement official required a notice to be delayed;
  · a contact at healdesk; and
  · any other available information Practice must include in a notice that a
    state breach-notification law requires of it.

  For anything else this clause covers, the report says what happened and
  when, what information was involved, and what healdesk has done about it.
  Who bears the cost of notifying clients, regulators and anyone else about a
  breach is allocated by the Liability clause.

13. CLIENTS' REQUESTS

  When a client asks Practice to see, correct or delete their Client
  Information, healdesk will give Practice what it needs to answer within ten
  business days of Practice asking. It will correct that information as
  Practice directs, and delete it as the clause headed "Where Practice is not
  a covered entity" provides. healdesk corrects a client's contact details in
  its own copy. It does not rewrite a booking request, an appointment record
  or the record of a conversation: it appends the correction to that record
  and links the two. Every later export of that record, and every screen of
  healdesk's that shows it, then carries the correction. When a client's
  information is deleted, healdesk keeps a record of the request that holds no
  telephone number, email address or name. For the 35 days in which a backup
  could still bring the information back, the record also holds one-way keyed
  references to the client's numbers, addresses and messaging accounts, and
  healdesk keeps Practice's own message asking for the deletion, both used
  only to delete the information again if a backup is restored; then both are
  removed.

  healdesk holds a transcript only to deliver it to Practice, and only for as
  long as the clause headed "Minimum necessary, and who receives Client
  Information" allows. Once the copy is deleted, healdesk has no transcript to
  give Practice, correct or delete: the email that carried it is Practice's
  own record, and Practice answers a request about a transcript from that
  email.

  If a client makes such a request, or any other request about their
  information, to healdesk itself rather than to Practice, healdesk will not
  act on it. It will pass the request to Practice within five business days
  and, in its reply to the client, tell them that healdesk cannot act on it
  and has passed it to Practice.

14. CONFIDENTIALITY

  healdesk will keep Client Information confidential. It will let only the
  people who need Client Information to provide the Services see it, and will
  bind each of them to keep it confidential.

15. WHAT HEALDESK IS NOT AUTHORISED TO DO

  · Use Client Information to train, fine-tune, or evaluate any artificial
    intelligence model, its own or a third party's.
  · Disclose Client Information to any advertising or analytics platform, in
    any form, including hashed or aggregated. Instagram, Messenger and
    WhatsApp are operated by Meta, which is not healdesk's subcontractor and
    is not bound by this agreement; where Practice connects them, the
    assistant replies there only inside a conversation the client started. It
    is instructed not to handle clinical content or confirm appointment
    details there and to move anything clinical to a call or to Practice's own
    secure route, and each reply the language model writes there is checked by
    automated filters designed to keep both out.
  · Provide clinical advice, diagnosis, triage, or treatment recommendations
    through the assistant.
  · Sell, license, or otherwise monetise Client Information.

16. ASSISTANCE AND AUDIT
